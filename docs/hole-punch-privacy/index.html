Hole Punch: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Hole Punch is a game for iPhone made by Tim White.

The short version

The game collects nothing about you.

What the game stores

Your progress, your best scores and your settings (sound and music on or off) are kept on your own device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking tools, ours or anyone else's.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The game offers one optional purchase, which unlocks further levels. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the game only whether the purchase has been made. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
